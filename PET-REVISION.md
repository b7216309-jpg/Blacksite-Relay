# Blacksite Relay 1.2.2 — unexpected company

Two cable rats, a sleepy server cat and a tiny copper-coil snail now inhabit the relay room. The rats pause, sniff, turn and scurry along the floor. The cat blinks and flicks an ear on its warm cabinet. The snail creeps across the other cabinet with gently moving eye stalks.

All three designs are original ImageGen artwork. Each animal uses one consistent drawing and small local movements; its body is never replaced by an unrelated pose. Sprites are sampled onto the native pixel grid, with contact shadows and lighting matched to the room.

The residents add no buttons, text, purchases or gameplay rules. Their clock belongs to the existing scene renderer. Mini-games, reduced motion, static scenes and hidden windows stop their animation. Four residents and four cached animation textures keep resource use bounded. No pet code reads saves, draws gameplay randomness or owns a timer.

The corrected 1.2.1 technician and the original looping music remain intact.

Editable sources: `src/relay-pets.js`, `assets/scene/relay-pets.json`, and `art-source/pets/`. Generation prompts and untouched source designs are included in the art archive.
