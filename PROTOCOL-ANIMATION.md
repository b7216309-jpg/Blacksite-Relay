# Protocol hardware animation — 1.3.1

The three minigame devices now have localized LED activity, core modulation and small electrical details in the same pixel-art style as their original backplates. The original generated PNGs are unchanged.

During active play, hardware activity stays subdued and outside protected commands, ports, gauges, clocks and controls. Brighter sparks and sweeps appear only during preparation or resolved feedback; a confirmed Bypass stop gives one brief contact spark. The Bypass cursor keeps its percentage-based timing, and Memory retains its exact port geometry and immediate reveal clearing.

The presentation uses its own clock and effect state. It does not resolve gameplay, call gameplay randomness or alter deadlines and rewards. Hidden/inactive devices, reduced motion and static mode stop added effects; low quality reduces their density. The room, residents, shadows, intro and original looping music are preserved.

Definitions and authoring notes are under `art-source/protocols/`. The delivered verification report records native/scaled inspection, actual keyboard outcomes, protected-pixel checks and repeated lifecycle testing.
