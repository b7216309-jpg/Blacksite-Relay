# Blacksite Relay 1.3.1 — hardware animation review

The three accepted 1.3.0 images were inspected at source resolution and in the real 420 × 650 compositions before placing the new effects. The animation is a separate native-resolution canvas; the original image files were not retouched.

The review covered all three live protocol screens, Bypass immediately after a successful keyboard stop, Injection with native typed input and its error state, and Memory during reveal and recall. It also covered deterministic quiet and peak hardware frames sampled over eight seconds at 24 samples per second, plus enlarged crops of the actual contact-spark locations. These samples were viewed as images; no claim of a subjective full-video or audio listen-through is made here. The encoded media and functional tests have their own verification records.

The strongest sampled overlays retain the underlying copper, glass and worn metal texture. The small spark shapes originate at hardware contacts. LEDs sit inside existing lamp faces, with at most a one-pixel attached spill. The old generic three-light rails have been removed from the protocols because they did not align with the painted lamps. Bypass's cyan bank and lower lens were repositioned against the native composition during review. Memory's phase-lamp effect stays to the right of the timer; the grid, row/column labels and reveal states remain visually separate from the copper-fin modulation.

During ordinary timed input, the lights and small surface modulation are subdued. Preparation and resolved phases permit brief contact sparks and copper-path pulses. Bypass has no preparation window, so a successful stop produces a short contact spark while the existing scanner continues immediately. No new text, input delay, challenge timer or target movement was added.

The refreshed preview retains three complete native 420 × 650 games beside a clean 3× hardware crop. The Memory close-up was moved to the lower-left board so it shows a lamp that actually animates. A trial trailer frame with the larger Memory hardware detail and readable portrait game was also inspected. Final runtime visuals were accepted before the release tests and final media capture.

The source registration notes and deterministic capture helpers are included with the animation authoring material. Test reports separately cover the protected control pixels, image preservation, reduced/off/hidden/inactive behavior, bounded scheduling, real keyboard outcomes, unchanged mechanics and release archives.
