HOW TO PLAY

01 / BREACH 123

Safe Entry: $5 / +3% risk. Standard Hack: $20 / +9%. Brute Force: $50 / +14%. These are base values; upgrades and multipliers change the outcome.

02 / TRACE

Each hack rolls against your trace risk. A higher risk means a greater chance of getting caught. At 100%, the next unprotected hack is certain to be detected. You can still transfer to escape. Ghost can absorb one trace.

03 / EXTRACT SPACE

Complete a hack, then transfer to secure your funds. Spend banked funds on permanent upgrades. Funds left in the relay can be lost.

04 / BYPASS B

Charge the bypass with 15 hacks, then stop the cursor inside the target zone with Space. Land at least two successful stops to earn 2 risk points of reduction per stop when the sequence ends. Each level gets harder. Escape exits and applies any earned reduction.

05 / BLACK MARKET

Q / Cooler removes 10 risk points. W / Insure protects 50% of funds for 5 hacks. E / Gamble can reward or bust you. R / Ghost absorbs one trace. A / Proxy removes 40% of current risk. S / Overclock doubles gain for 2 hacks and increases current risk by 50%.

06 / SPECIAL PROTOCOLS

Type a revealed command before time expires, or memorize the five illuminated cells and find them again. These protocols pause your normal controls.

07 / PRESTIGE

Prestige resets banked funds and upgrades to grant permanent gain, risk reduction, and starting funds. Its next cost appears in your loadout.
