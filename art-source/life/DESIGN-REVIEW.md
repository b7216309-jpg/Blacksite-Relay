# Character correction criteria

The rejected 1.2.0 character mixed six separate illustrations. Its outline, collar and proportions changed between poses; the headset arm appeared without intermediate movement. Its hand pointed at a reactor housing rather than a control. Fractional downsampling reduced each imported pixel to about 0.53 native screen pixels. Clean compositing did not establish good character design or animation.

The replacement is a civilian technician with a handheld diagnostic pad, broad readable cloth shapes and controlled native pixel placement. A geometric Blender study preserved anatomy but looked too much like a mannequin, so the final animation uses one drawn figure with connected motion. The lower body stays fixed.

An independent motion review caught gray edge residue and a breathing pop. Background-connected matte cleanup removed the residue, and quiet coat shading replaced geometric breathing. The corrected 180-frame capture has identical lower-body pixels throughout; all 72 idle frames retain the same silhouette. Head/wrist gestures stay connected, and cool tablet marks help distinguish its task.

The corrected character was inspected in the original room at 420×150 and enlarged. This is a bounded visual review, not an automatic quality score or a claim that every artistic preference is settled.
