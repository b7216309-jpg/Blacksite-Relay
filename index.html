Income scales with your multiplier, Miner, prestige and an active Overclock. VPN and prestige reduce the added risk; Overvolt can make a hack add none.

Risk checks happen first

A hack checks your existing trace risk before adding its own. Below 20 risk, detection chance is 70% of your actual risk; from 20 onward, it matches that risk.

100% is your last warning.

The next unprotected hack is certain to be detected. You can still transfer or lower your risk first.

Build momentum

Successful hacks advance a shared streak. Safe Entry or Standard raises the multiplier once that streak reaches 2; Brute Force needs 3. Completing a node adds another +1 multiplier and removes 5 risk points.

Random events can change your funds, risk or multiplier after a hack. Read the result before your next move.

Secure the run

SPACE transfers after at least one successful hack. Busting loses the unsecured balance; active Insurance recovers half. Your permanent bank stays safe.

Bonuses spend unsecured funds. The price on each card is current. Waiting, shopping and protocols do not advance hack-based timers.

Q COOLER −10 RISK POINTS

Remove 10 risk points. Price doubles each purchase; Cooling Fan reduces its base cost. Unavailable at zero risk.

W INSURE 5 HACKS

Pay 20% of your funds. If busted during coverage, bank half of what remains. The fifth hack is covered; cannot stack or refresh.

E GAMBLE 5-HACK COOLDOWN

After your first successful hack, pay 20% for a random cash or risk outcome. It can instantly bust. Deep Learning improves the odds.

Exact Gamble odds

At Deep Learning level L (0–4): jackpot 1+L%; lower risk 38%; add risk 23%; lose fee 28%; purge 1%; bust 9−L%.

After the fee: jackpot doubles remaining funds and removes 15 risk points; lower risk removes 20; add risk adds 10; purge clears risk. Ghost can absorb a fatal result; Insurance covers an actual bust.

R GHOST ONE TRACE

Absorb one hack detection or fatal Gamble and clear current risk. A protected hack then adds its normal risk. Rebuy after the shield is spent and 5 hacks have passed. Price triples.

A PROXY −40% CURRENT RISK

Keep 60% of your current risk. Price doubles each purchase and rises with completed nodes. Unavailable at zero risk.

S OVERCLOCK 2 HACKS

Pay 15% of your funds. Double hack income for 2 hacks; immediately multiply current risk by 1.5 (up to 100). Rebuy after 7 hacks.

Ghost + Overclock cannot overlap.

Once Overclock's 2-hack boost ends, Ghost is allowed during its remaining cooldown. Buffs, cooldowns and escalating prices reset each run.

Normal actions pause while a protocol has focus. Injection and Memory timers keep running if you leave the window. Prepare, then commit.

BYPASS B

Charge with 15 successful hacks per use. Press SPACE or click Stop Scanner inside the target zone. Gets faster with narrower targets.

From 2 successful stops onward, earn 2 risk points of reduction per stop. ESC exits; exit or miss applies earned reduction, down to zero risk.

INJECTION 7 SECONDS

A random challenge after hacking. After 3 seconds to prepare, type the command within 7 seconds. Case does not matter; use Backspace to correct it.

A fourth simultaneous mismatched character fails. Success adds a multiplier bonus equal to completed nodes (minimum +1); failure adds 10 risk points.

MEMORY 8 SECONDS

A random challenge after hacking. Memorize 5 lit ports for 3 seconds, then find them within 8. Click, or move with arrow keys and choose with ENTER or SPACE. A third wrong port fails.

Success adds funds equal to 3 × your multiplier and removes 25 risk points. Failure adds 15 risk points.

Injection or Memory failure raises risk.

It does not itself trigger a trace or consume Ghost. Injection and Memory cannot be skipped.
